Inadequate breathing · bag-mask ventilationPractice ventilationWatch ventilation · 77 s
Chest pressure · 12-lead ECG acquisitionPractice ECGWatch ECG · 98 s

Ventilation practice covers a two-person role split, mask placement, assisted breaths, seal-loss recovery and reassessment. ECG practice covers electrode placement, acquisition quality, contact correction and a supported report; it does not teach diagnostic interpretation.

These skills appear in the national EMT education standards ↗. Local scope and instructor guidance apply. Findings are scripted; virtual performance does not establish clinical competence. Curriculum review and missing skills · Procedure audit
