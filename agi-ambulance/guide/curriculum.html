EMT curriculum and scope

The current offering contains two limited practice candidates: bag-mask ventilation and 12-lead ECG acquisition. Both address subjects in US national EMT education; neither is a validated competency assessment or certification course.

Practice ventilation · Practice ECG acquisition · Watch the procedures

Why these cases remain

The ventilation case connects findings and partner coordination with airway position, mask support, attempted breaths, an authored seal fault, physical correction, reassessment and handover. Its response is qualitative and scripted, rather than a measurement of ventilation mechanics.

The ECG case includes preparation, ten individually placed electrodes, lead connections, synthetic recording quality, contact correction and repeat observations. National guidance includes ECG acquisition/transmission at EMT level; interpretation is a separate competency. State scope, education and agency authorization govern actual practice, and ECG acquisition is not part of every EMT practice setting.

Equipment handling is a subtask

The former ten standalone equipment workflows have been retired. Fetching a kit, closing an empty-cot buckle, replacing a canister lid or powering a monitor did not demonstrate the patient-care outcomes named by those lessons.

Equipment remains interactive in free exploration. Preparation and readiness matter, but a training task needs evidence such as a functional check, suitable supplies, patient response or securement—not merely an object changing state.

What remains to build

Patient assessment with measurement interactions, oxygen support, suction function and use, external hemorrhage control, CPR/AED teamwork, patient packaging and transport safety, and patient handover remain development priorities. They are not additional playable cases or a ranking of the most frequent procedures.

Future tasks should begin with the required outcome, meaningful errors and recovery. Missing patient features, equipment and mechanisms must then be authored in Blender before a lightweight browser version is made.

Reference framework

  • National EMS Education Standards ↗: educational domains and competencies, including assessment, ventilation, resuscitation, trauma and operations.
  • National EMS Scope of Practice Model with 2021 changes ↗: national level distinctions, including ECG acquisition and transmission.
  • NREMT EMT Examination Specifications ↗: assessment, treatment, reassessment, transport, communication and equipment readiness as distinct job tasks.

These references guide the project's curriculum decisions. They do not imply that the issuing organizations have evaluated or endorsed this simulator.
