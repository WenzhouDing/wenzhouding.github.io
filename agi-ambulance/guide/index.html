Using the ambulance

Explore the reconstructed cabin, practice one of two patient cases, or watch its procedure demonstration. The browser scene is a lightweight version of Blender-authored equipment and mechanisms.

Open the simulator · Procedure demonstrations · EMT curriculum and scope

Desktop controls

  • Drag to look around; use WASD to walk.
  • Click an object, or select it from Equipment, to see its actions.
  • E operates the selected control or the held item's primary action.
  • G picks up or releases a movable item. The mouse wheel changes holding distance.
  • Return puts the selected item home. Reset restores the room.

In free exploration, try the cabinet doors, buckles, nets, plugs, handset and bags. These interactions demonstrate equipment mechanisms; they do not earn clinical task credit. Carrying, gravity and deformation use simplified models, without comprehensive object collision or full fabric simulation.

Patient cases

Assisted ventilation concerns an adult with inadequate breathing and a pulse. Use the case panel for findings and partner coordination; the physical task includes airway and mask positioning, attempted breaths, correction and reassessment.

ECG acquisition concerns a fictional adult with chest pressure. Use the case panel alongside preparation, electrode placement, lead connections, recording and contact correction. Acquiring a recording is separate from interpreting it.

The simulator's Clinical case selector changes cases. Watch procedure loads a demonstration; Free exploration leaves the case or replay. Case feedback describes the simulated evidence collected, not a clinical qualification.

WebXR controllers

Use a compatible immersive WebXR browser over HTTPS and select Enter XR. A headset must be available to the browser.

  • Point and press trigger to select equipment, operate a control or activate an in-world panel button.
  • Hold grip near a movable item, or while pointing at it, to carry it. Release grip to let go.
  • Use the thumbstick for stepped movement and snap turns. Return it to neutral between steps.
  • Thumbstick click or the primary face button hides or restores the panel. Trigger away from equipment can reopen a hidden panel.

The in-world panel provides case selection, instructions, actions and replay controls. Controllers are the supported input approach; physical-headset ergonomics and performance still need device testing. There is one held-object slot, and the app does not provide a physical room safety boundary.
