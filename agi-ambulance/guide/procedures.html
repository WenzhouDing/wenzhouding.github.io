Assisted ventilation

The 77-second demonstration shows an inspection/refill subset, airway opening, mask support, assisted bag and chest motion, oxygen connection, an ineffective breath, physical mask reseating and continuing support through handover.

This is a limited adult, nontraumatic case with a pulse. The equipment inspection segment is not a complete manufacturer function check. The scripted bag-squeezing partner and qualitative chest response do not measure pressure, delivered volume, oxygen concentration or a real mask seal.

ECG acquisition

The 98-second demonstration shows preparation, ten electrode placements with connected lead travel, acquisition, a V2 contact fault, lift and reapplication, recording and a repeat with consistent placement.

The ECG records and patient observations are synthetic. The demonstration concerns acquisition and contact correction; it does not establish diagnostic proficiency or demonstrate full chest-pain treatment.

Playback controls

  • Play/Pause, or Space on desktop, controls playback.
  • Drag the timeline to inspect motion forward or backward.
  • Previous/Next step and the chapter list jump to a phase and pause.
  • Playback speed changes viewing speed only; it is not a clinical timing recommendation.
  • Follow the procedure camera is optional. Frame this step adjusts the desktop view once.
  • Read the chapter's findings and source links alongside the scene. Pause to read longer passages.

In WebXR, select a demonstration from the in-world menu. Its panel offers playback, chapter navigation, speed, case details and exit. Replay does not move the tracked headset camera.

Practice a fresh case starts a new learner episode. Exit replay returns to exploration. Watching, scrubbing and chapter selection never count as learner actions or completed treatment.
